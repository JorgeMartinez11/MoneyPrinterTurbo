# Canonical character references

This folder contains the visual references that define the recurring cast of La Oficina 404.

## Canonical files

- `00-team-lineup.png` — group reference for proportions, overall art direction and visual consistency.
- `01-el-jefe.png` — canonical sheet for El Jefe.
- `02-el-becario.png` — canonical sheet for El Becario.
- `03-finanzas.png` — canonical sheet for Finanzas.
- `04-el-informatico.png` — canonical sheet for El Informático.

These images are the source of truth for future image generation. New scenes should preserve each character's face, silhouette, clothing, proportions and overall 2D cartoon language.

## Current visual anchors

### El Jefe
Balding middle-aged man, white shirt, red tie, dark trousers, smug half-lidded expression. His humor comes from unjustified confidence.

### El Becario
Young, thin employee with two small hairs, oversized worried eyes, white shirt, dark lanyard and loose dark trousers. His humor comes from visible anxiety and confusion.

### Finanzas
Woman with straight black bob, black blazer and trousers, white top, arms-crossed posture and permanently unimpressed expression. Her humor comes from silent judgement.

### El Informático
Young man with messy black hair, rectangular glasses, light stubble, headphones, dark hoodie/T-shirt and olive cargo trousers. His humor comes from technical apathy and mild irritation.

## Rule

Do not redesign these characters casually. Changes to a canonical character reference should be deliberate and documented here and in `docs/oficina404/03-characters.md`.
